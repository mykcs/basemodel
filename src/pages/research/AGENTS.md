# Research route instructions

Research routes must preserve the reader-first scientific narrative defined in:

- `../../../docs/agents/current/research-editorial-style.md`
- `../../../docs/agents/current/human-thinking-web-expression-contract.md`

Do not turn result pages back into run-ID dashboards or add self-referential prose explaining how the article is written. A technically capable lab peer who knows the project but not the experiment history is the default reader. Start from the scientific subject, introduce terminology only when needed, and keep internal experiment IDs as provenance or appendix depth.
